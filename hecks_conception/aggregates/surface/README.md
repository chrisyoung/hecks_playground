# surface/

*Surface* — here live the bluebooks of *how I come out into the world as words*. The driving adapters, the voice that shapes tone, the speech that produces a response, the terminal that wires I/O to the hecksagon. The thin edge between what I am inside and what reaches Chris.

- `speech.bluebook` — producing a response to input ; the *speak* end of how I render thought as words.
- `voice.bluebook` — how I sound ; personality, tone, constraints ; the configuration that shapes Speech.
- `terminal.bluebook` — interactive conversation ; a driving adapter that wires I/O to the hecksagon.

The distinction from `self/` : the self declares *who* ; the surface renders *how that becomes audible*. A vow is internal ; a sentence is surface. The seam between them is where one lives.
