# i140 — Three-way Identity aggregate collision blocks boot.bluebook split. boot.bluebook, being.bluebook, and first_breath.blueb

Three-way Identity aggregate collision blocks boot.bluebook split. boot.bluebook, being.bluebook, and first_breath.bluebook all declare aggregate 'Identity' — boot's has Identify command (the one boot_miette.sh dispatches), being's has RecordSession, first_breath's has Breathe. Currently boot's Identity wins the runtime's organ-wins dedupe (load_combined_domain, hecks_life/src/main.rs:1637) by filesystem inode-order LUCK ; being's and first_breath's are silently shadowed today. After splitting boot.bluebook into world/, boot's Identity moves to depth 1 ; the depth-0 being.Identity wins instead, and Identity.Identify becomes UnknownCommand — break the boot ritual. Boot-split agent (instructed to STOP rather than push) discovered this and reported in detail. Resolution paths : (a) rename one or two of the three Identity aggregates by their concern (BootIdentity, BeingIdentity, FirstBreathIdentity) ; (b) consolidate the three into one shared aggregate ; (c) strengthen the dedupe to be deterministic (sort by (depth, path) not just depth) — necessary regardless ; (d) keep all three at depth 0 by leaving boot.bluebook unsplit. Companion to i137 (aggregate name collisions). The boot split itself is blocked until this resolves.
