# i408 — Design: fatigue_state ladder should be threshold-gated on pulses_since_sleep, not a step-per-tick lifecycle. Observed 20

Design: fatigue_state ladder should be threshold-gated on pulses_since_sleep, not a step-per-tick lifecycle. Observed 2026-04-20: after a full 8-cycle sleep + successful wake (cycle=8/8, mood=refreshed, pulses reset to 0, fatigue to 0.0), Miette hit 'delirious' within 5 ticks because the body.bluebook Heartbeat lifecycle walks fatigue_state alert→focused→normal→tired→exhausted→delirious one rung per AccumulateFatigue, and AccumulateFatigue fires on every Ticked (1Hz). Five ticks after wake = delirious. Quick fix landed (commit TBD): removed lifecycle transitions on AccumulateFatigue; added Walk* commands guarded by pulses_since_sleep thresholds (250/500/1000/1400/1800) + matching policies on FatigueAccumulated. Proper design: consider making fatigue_state a read-time computed view of pulses_since_sleep rather than persistent, so the ladder is always consistent with counters and can't drift. Fits the inbox #2 (fatigue→mood transitions) work.
