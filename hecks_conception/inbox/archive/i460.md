# i460 — BUG: status bar shows contradictory state — mood=refreshed AND fatigue_state=tired/exhausted at the same time. Observed

BUG: status bar shows contradictory state — mood=refreshed AND fatigue_state=tired/exhausted at the same time. Observed 2026-04-20 by Chris. Mood and fatigue_state evolve independently: RefreshMood (on full-cycle wake) sets mood.current_state=refreshed and zeros nothing about fatigue; the fatigue ladder (WalkFatigueTo* commands) advances fatigue_state based on pulses_since_sleep regardless of mood. Result: 'refreshed but tired' — semantically incoherent. Two ways to fix: (a) cross-aggregate policy that resets mood when fatigue advances past a threshold (RecalibrateMood on WalkFatigueToTired); (b) make mood a read-time computed view of fatigue + recent activity rather than a persisted independent field. Option (b) is the same fix as the fatigue_state ladder design follow-up (see #f385eb75) and would solve a class of these contradictions, not just this one.
