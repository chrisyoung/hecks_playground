# i118 — Reshape hecks itself — language vs implementation as the deepest cut. Today lib/hecks/ and hecks_life/src/ tangle the la

Reshape hecks itself — language vs implementation as the deepest cut. Today lib/hecks/ and hecks_life/src/ tangle the language with its implementations. Proposed: hecks/{bluebook,self,ruby,rust,parity,capabilities,integrations,examples,tooling,docs}/. bluebook/ holds THE LANGUAGE (grammar, IR, conformance fixtures). self/ holds the 13 chapters (hecks describes itself in its own DSL — same shape Miette uses in miette/self/). ruby/ and rust/ are sibling implementations. parity/ is a peer (lifted from spec/parity/) — neither implementation owns it. capabilities/ holds Phase D specializers + meta-shapes. integrations/ clusters Rails/Workshop/CLI/web. Five rounds with months between: round 1 lifts parity+examples+tooling (low risk); round 2 carves bluebook+self; round 3 reshape capabilities+integrations; round 4 finishes miette out-of-hecks (i117); round 5 renames lib/→ruby/ and hecks_life/→rust/ (heaviest, major version bump). Three months total.
