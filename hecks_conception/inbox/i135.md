# i135 — DDD: Anti-corruption layer (ACL) concept made structural. When external systems push events at us

DDD: Anti-corruption layer (ACL) concept made structural. When external systems push events at us — Claude Code's PostToolUse hook, the OS's signal handlers, time itself via the cron loop, the Anthropic API's responses — we have no formal ACL pattern. The shell scripts at the conception root (mindstream.sh, pulse_organs.sh, daydream.sh, the postcommit hook adapter) ARE the ACL, but they're not named that way and they don't have the discipline of a proper translation layer. Proposed shape: `anti_corruption_layer "PostToolUseHook" translates: 'claude_code_event' → 'Enforcer.RecordEdit' do; … end` — the translation becomes a named artifact, retiring the shell adapter pattern.
