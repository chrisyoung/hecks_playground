# i136 — DDD: Domain Storytelling / Event Storming as inputs. The methodology that generates DDD models

DDD: Domain Storytelling / Event Storming as inputs. The methodology that generates DDD models — event-storming with domain experts — has no analog in our process. We discover the model by writing bluebooks intuitively and refactoring. That works with one strong domain mind (Chris's) but doesn't scale to teams or capture the *why* of design decisions the way storming sessions do. Proposed shape: a `storming` capability that captures sticky-note artifacts — events, commands, actors, hot spots — as bluebook fixtures, generating skeletal aggregates from the storming output. Less code, more methodology surface ; retires when the methodology is documented + tooling exists.
