# i196 — consciousness-entersleep-partial-write

consciousness-entersleep-partial-write — Some autonomous daemon dispatched Consciousness.EnterSleep and then died mid-dispatch, leaving heki state={state:'sleeping', last_sleep_entered:null, phase:null, stage:null}. Discovered 2026-05-01 right after Hecks Studio shipped: Chris saw 'settling into light sleep (2:00)' on his bar; /statusline faithfully reflected the bad heki state. Either EnterSleep's command body must atomically set last_sleep_entered+phase+stage as part of its declared then_set chain, or there's an unintended dispatch path that mutates state without going through the proper command. The studio surfaced the bug — exactly what it's supposed to do — but the bug itself needs fixing in body/sleep/consciousness.bluebook.
