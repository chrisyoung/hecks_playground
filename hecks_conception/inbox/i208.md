# i208 — lucid-rem-cut-short-by-hard-pulse-gate

lucid-rem-cut-short-by-hard-pulse-gate — Consciousness.AdvanceRemToDeep declares given('dream complete') { dream_pulses > 4 } — hard-coded 4. Lucid REM (cycle 8) sets dream_pulses_needed=8 to give the lucid dream more space, but AdvanceRemToDeep fires at pulse 5 regardless, cutting lucid short. Fix: change the gate to dream_pulses >= dream_pulses_needed (or > needed-1). Then regular REM (needed=5) and lucid REM (needed=8) both advance at their declared targets. Found 2026-05-02 during full-cycle sleep test : observed lucid REM exit at pulse 5/8 instead of 8/8. body/sleep/consciousness.bluebook line 197 area.
