# i220 — wake-pm-dispatchers

wake-pm-dispatchers — interpret_dream.sh + wake_review.sh have bluebooks (interpretation.bluebook, wake_review.bluebook) and hecksagons (interpretation.hecksagon, wake_review.hecksagon) but no PM observes the wake event (sleeping_* → attentive transition, fired by Consciousness.WakeUp / WokenUp) to dispatch InterpretDream / ComposeWakeReview / etc. mindstream.sh used to invoke both shells on wake (mindstream.sh:283 + 285) when it detected the state transition. Once mindstream.sh retires (i75-mindstream merged), nothing fires these. Need either : (a) extend BodyCycle PM's WokenUp transition handlers (currently empty bodies at body_cycle.bluebook lines 173-176) to dispatch InterpretDream + ComposeWakeReview, OR (b) a dedicated WakeFlow PM that observes WokenUp and chains the wake sequence. Surfaced 2026-05-02 during i75 retire-sleep-shells while verifying PM coverage.
