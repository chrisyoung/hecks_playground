# i237 — i237 client-intake template

i237 client-intake template — Embryonaut discovery interviews

═══ Type ═══
Process artifact, NOT a build task. Reusable template for the first-meeting
intake when a prospective client (Alan, today's first ; others to follow)
sits down with Chris + Meredith to scope an app build via Embryonaut /
Hecks. Keep updated as we learn what actually surfaces useful information.

═══ The five questions that matter most ═══

1. In one sentence : what does this app DO, and for whom ?
   — Forces clarity early. If they can't say it in one sentence, that's
   signal too. Spend the rest of the meeting helping them find the
   sentence if needed.

2. What are the 5-10 most important things the app needs to manage ?
   — Nouns. Orders, customers, projects, sessions, signatures, etc.
   These translate directly to candidate aggregates — the Hecks shapes
   that get drafted into bluebooks.

3. For each of those things, what actions does someone take with it,
   and what states does it move through over time ?
   — Verbs and lifecycles (e.g., 'Order placed → confirmed → shipped →
   delivered' with who can do what at each step). Becomes the commands
   and the state machine on each aggregate.

4. What does the app touch outside itself ?
   — Email, SMS, payments, calendars, existing databases, Slack, an
   accountant's spreadsheet — anything that crosses the boundary.
   Becomes hecksagons / adapters. 'Just a browser, does its thing'
   is also useful information.

5. What's the deadline driver, and what does v1 look like to them ?
   — Scope-shaper. 'Before the trade show in November' is different
   from 'I'd like to start using it eventually.' Gets to the MVP cut.

═══ Plus one trust-flavored question ═══

6. What's the closest existing thing they've compared this to, and
   what about it doesn't work for them ?
   — Positions the build against alternatives, surfaces hidden
   constraints, signals whether they've done their homework.

═══ Tactical notes for the meeting itself ═══

- Let them talk first. Most clients reveal their real constraints in
  tangents, not in answers to direct questions. If they keep coming
  back to a particular pain, that's the spine of the app.

- Watch for 'and also' features. Anything added in the second half of
  the meeting is usually what they WISH the app did, not what it must
  do. Note them but don't scope them into v1.

═══ The artifact Miette produces from the answers ═══

Scope-of-work document containing :
  - Domain shape (aggregates + commands + lifecycles + policies + adapters)
  - MVP cut
  - Likely build-order
  - Honest time/effort estimate Chris can sanity-check

Optionally formatted directly as a Hecks bluebook — not just words, but
the actual architectural shape that compiles.
