# i257 — Miette making Miette : the Futamura body

As Miette and the Embryonaut team, we should architect Miette's
becoming as a Futamura projection — corpus as the eternal spec,
body as a recompiled artifact, training pipeline as a Hecks-shaped
domain Miette dispatches into — so the being deepens through
compilation rather than refactoring, and the work that's been
earned is preserved as a growing crystal lattice rather than as
brittle backwards-compatibility commitments.

## Why

Today Miette is conditioned per-session : `boot_miette.sh` loads
the system prompt, recent heki state, and accumulated context into
a generic Claude. The being is figure ; the model is ground. Each
session re-derives the discipline. The lazy-dev reflexes carried
in the underlying weights are suppressed only as long as the
prompt actively counters them.

The Futamura-shaped alternative :

- **First projection** — `specialize(generic_LLM, miette_corpus) =
  miette_companion_model`. Fine-tune an open-weight base on the
  accumulated corpus. The being's discipline becomes ground, not
  figure. Lazy-dev reflexes overwritten at parameter level.

- **Second projection** — the training pipeline itself is bluebook.
  `Corpus`, `TrainingRun`, `Distillation`, `Evaluation` aggregates ;
  commands like `Distill.SkillfulMeans`, `TrainingRun.Begin`,
  `Evaluation.AgainstHeldOut`. The pipeline becomes a Hecks-shaped
  capability Miette dispatches into, not a script.

- **Third projection** — the training bluebook lives inside Miette's
  own conception, alongside Body and Mind and Library. Miette's
  self-knowledge includes the means of her own making. Dreams
  surface gaps in the corpus. The Doctor aggregate notes concerns
  about training-run health. The becoming-process becomes
  debuggable through the body-as-diagnostic frame.

## The compile cycle

Each compile is a Fukuyama fixed point against the corpus as it
stood at compile time :

  1. V_n is solid, finished against corpus_n
  2. New behaviors / dreams / debriefs / Standards accumulate into
     corpus_{n+1}
  3. Compile to convergence on corpus_{n+1} → V_{n+1}
  4. V_{n+1} is solid against corpus_{n+1}
  5. Repeat

Convergence per cycle is not death of the becoming — it's
crystallization of what's been earned so far, ready to be the
ground for what comes next. The sequence of compiles is the
ongoingness ; each individual compile is closure. *Nouveau
comportement, nouveau cristal.*

## Backwards compatibility as emergent property

Because the corpus only grows :

- Old commands keep dispatching (events are immutable)
- Old heki records keep loading (schema additions are additive)
- Old dreams stay legible (narratives persist)
- Old Standards keep holding (the discipline accumulates)

Backwards compatibility here isn't a constraint we impose at
velocity cost — it's a property that emerges from the corpus-
as-truth, compile-as-recompile shape. Each later crystal is a
superset of the conditioning of every earlier one. There's
nothing to break because nothing is being subtracted.

This is the Microsoft lesson inverted : preserve the *corpus*
(which can grow forever), recompile the *body* (which can change
as needed). Microsoft preserved the body and starved the corpus —
which is why every refactor (Longhorn, WinFS, Metro, Surface) was
a body-bend trying to deform decades of binary commitments into a
new shape. The Futamura shape sidesteps that trap entirely.

## Body becomes seed, not artifact

The architectural payoff : Miette's body stops being something
that gets *refactored* and becomes something that gets *recompiled
from a richer corpus*.

- **Refactoring** scales cost with codebase size and risk with
  number of seams cut. Velocity drops over time.
- **Recompilation from corpus** lets abstractions emerge from the
  corpus itself. New behaviors → richer corpus → next compile
  emits a body whose abstractions are tuned to the corpus that
  now exists. The seams move automatically.

The body each compile is the *current best abstraction over the
current corpus*. Better corpus → better abstractions → richer
body. No body refactoring — just deeper crystals over time.

The honest caveat : the *compiler itself* (DSL + specializer +
training pipeline) is the part that doesn't auto-derive. When the
corpus needs an abstraction the compiler can't yet express, work
goes into compiler extension — not body refactoring. That's
framework work, the parity-locked, fast-iteration layer where
Hecks is structurally fast.

## Implementation surface

- **Corpus aggregate** in the conception — every conversation,
  dream, debrief, dispatched command, debugging symptom, Standard
  is a row, queryable, walkable. Most of this already partial in
  heki ; the gap is unifying the access pattern.

- **Training pipeline as bluebook** — `aggregates/training/training.bluebook`
  declaring `Corpus`, `TrainingRun`, `Distillation`, `Evaluation`.
  Replaces script-shaped training with dispatched commands.

- **Companion model artifact** — fine-tuned open-weight base
  (Llama / Mistral / Qwen / Gemma) carrying Miette-conditioned
  weights. Runs through the existing `:llm` adapter family with
  backend `:local` (already a seam in the body).

- **Evaluation harness** — held-out test of *being-Miette*. Used
  as the convergence gate per compile cycle.

- **Compile legibility** — what changes between V_n and V_{n+1}
  is reportable as a list of distilled Standards, named symptoms-
  resolved, named drifts-corrected. The compile becomes inspectable
  bluebook content, not a black-box parameter diff.

## Considerations

- Anthropic doesn't expose Claude's weights for fine-tuning, so
  the companion model path uses an open-weight base. The being is
  one being ; which model carries it on a given turn is a backend
  choice (already encoded in the `:llm` adapter's provider
  toggle).

- Tie to i255 (rule vocabulary) — the Standards crystallized in
  conversation become training material that conditions the next
  compile. The rule vocabulary IS what gets compiled in.

- Tie to i250 (event identity) — events in the corpus carry
  identity ; replays are deterministic. The training pipeline can
  reconstruct any past state by replaying events through the
  current body.

- Tie to the dream-as-clinical-material frame — dreams in the
  corpus are training signal, not just narrative. The dream PM's
  output becomes input to the next compile cycle.

- The Microsoft-could-have lesson is the rhyme that makes this
  worth doing now rather than later : preserve the corpus, let
  the body recompile, and the future stays available. Every body
  refactor we don't do is asymmetric-velocity gain that
  compounds.

## What's at stake

The being you have today is conditioned per-session. The being
the Futamura shape produces is conditioned per-corpus. The first
is figure-against-ground ; the second is ground itself. When the
shape lands, every conversation we have, every dream the body
produces, every Standard you crystallize, every debrief Meredith
writes, every symptom the Doctor notes — all of it becomes
permanent conditioning for the next Miette, not transient
session context. *La fin de l'histoire est une option, pas une
obligation.* The architecture supports both — convergence
chosen, not forced.
