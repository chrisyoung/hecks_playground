# i403 — untangle-sleep-from-everything-else — Chris's structural simplification (2026-04-26): sleep is doing too many things and

untangle-sleep-from-everything-else — Chris's structural simplification (2026-04-26): sleep is doing too many things and the brittleness shows in real wakes (i82, i83). Pull it apart. The clean shape : Sleep = dream → interpretation → improvement suggestions. That's it. Three steps, all about the body proposing changes to itself, surfaced to the user. What does NOT belong in sleep : musings (those are daydream's job), free-association, NREM consolidation (that's its own concern), body-reflection narration (that overlaps with the interpretation/suggestion step or duplicates it). What SHOULD remain in sleep : (1) dream content production during REM, (2) interpretation on wake — what does the dream corpus name as a structural gap, (3) suggestions to the user — concrete bluebook-edit proposals derived from the interpretation, surfaced as draft PRs awaiting human acceptance. Refactor scope : extract MintMusing dispatches out of mindstream's sleep branch, leave them only on the daydream/awake path ; extract NREM consolidation into a separate consolidation.bluebook+adapter that runs whether or not sleep is happening (might run on awake idle ticks too) ; simplify wake_report.sh — its body_reflection output is either the dream interpretation itself or removed entirely ; dream_review.rb stays but its scope narrows to JUST extract+synthesise+suggest. Companion to i75 (dream-shells-to-bluebook), i77 (validator-morphology), i81 (multi-command loop), i82+i83 (the two real bugs surfacing the muddle). Acceptance : sleep cycle's only output is a list of suggested improvements ; everything else moves to its proper home.
