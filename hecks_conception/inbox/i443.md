# i443 — Bluebooks today carry a single `category:` string. Domains often belong to several categories at once (e.g. a telemedici

Bluebooks today carry a single `category:` string. Domains often belong to several categories at once (e.g. a telemedicine bluebook is both `healthcare` AND `saas` AND `consumer`). Expand the field to `categories:` (multi-value list) and backfill ~303 existing files with their current single value as a one-element list.

Migration shape:
- DSL: `categories ["healthcare", "saas"]` (list form); `category "x"` kept as sugar that upserts into the list
- IR: aggregate-level `categories: Vec<String>`; deprecate singular `category` field
- Builders: Ruby + Rust both accept either form, emit the list form canonically
- Backfill: one-shot migration script wrapping `hecks-life` rewrite; ~303 files touched (current single-category count)

Why it matters: the category taxonomy i27 (NurseryHealth) and i42 (catalog dialect) both assume crisp single classification. Real domains don't. Moving to multi-value makes both of those capabilities more useful and unblocks cross-cutting queries.

Pairs with i27 (nursery viability / NurseryHealth capability) and i42 (catalog dialect).
