# i488 — `role` keyword collides with `reference_to`'s legacy `role:` kwarg

Surfaced 2026-05-07 by the i483 agent while drafting Agent + Role.
The DSL reference at §References notes :

```ruby
reference_to "Pizza", role: :home_team    # legacy kwarg form
```

is accepted alongside the canonical `as: :home_team`. The new
i483 `role Role, as: Agent` shape doesn't ambiguate at the parser
level (different position in the AST — top-level call inside a
`command` block vs. kwarg of `reference_to`), but the semantic
overlap is real.

## Resolution

When the parser-support card for the new `role` form lands, two
related cleanups :

1. **Migration story note** : the eventual mechanical sweep should
   not rewrite legacy `reference_to ..., role:` calls into the new
   `role Role, as: ...` form — they mean different things.
2. **Optional : retire the legacy `role:` kwarg on `reference_to`** in
   favour of the canonical `as:`. Standards say no backward compat
   until first user — bin-buddy is the closest thing to a first
   user, so worth one pass to clean.
