# i532 — Five adapter bluebooks fail / warn at validate

## Why this card exists

The adapters/ consolidation pass on 2026-05-11 (commits `21650950`
+ `5716497e`) tried to copy every adapter-shaped bluebook into the
new categorized layout. Five originals failed `storehouse validate`
or surfaced design warnings ; the subagent (correctly) refused to
propagate broken files into the new home and noted the gap in each
category README. Until these are fixed, the adapters/ entries for
them are README-only stubs pointing back at the original path.

## The five

| Location | Status | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| `hecks_conception/adapters/heki.bluebook` | INVALID — 11 errors | **error** |
| `hecks_conception/adapters/ollama.bluebook` | INVALID — 9 errors | **error** |
| `integrations/web_components/web_components.bluebook` | INVALID — 7 errors | **error** |
| `integrations/cloudflare_deploy/cloudflare_deploy.bluebook` | warning — 62 structural units | warning |
| `integrations/web_application_creation/web_application_creation.bluebook` | warning — 6 disconnected concern clusters | warning |

The three INVALID ones almost certainly trip on the
primitive-String-instead-of-value-object rule (the same one i102
tracks). The two warnings are design-shape issues : a bluebook
with 62 structural units is probably trying to be two bluebooks ;
six disconnected concern clusters likewise.

## Why it matters

Every adapter is supposed to declare its UL as a clean bluebook.
When the framework's own adapter bluebooks don't validate, the
"everything is bluebook" promise has visible holes. Customer-side
bluebooks may reference these adapters and inherit the brokenness
silently. The validator is the gate ; broken originals upstream
mean downstream callers are working off invalid declarations.

## Proposed fix order

1. **heki.bluebook** — kernel-floor, every storage-backed
   aggregate sits on it. Highest blast radius. Wrap primitives
   in value-objects ; same shape as the recently-built r2.bluebook
   (which validates cleanly). Estimated : 30-45 minutes.

2. **ollama.bluebook** — similar shape ; wrap. Estimated : 20
   minutes.

3. **web_components.bluebook** — UI component declarations ; fix
   the 7 errors. Estimated : 30 minutes.

4. **cloudflare_deploy.bluebook** — split into two bluebooks if
   the warning is right that there are two concerns. Bigger
   surgery ; do after the first three are clean.

5. **web_application_creation.bluebook** — six disconnected
   clusters means the bluebook is six bluebooks pretending to
   be one. Largest split ; do last.

After each fix, copy the now-clean bluebook into its `adapters/`
home and update the category README. The retirement contract :
once adapters/ has the canonical copies, the originals can be
deleted in a second pass.

## Companion work

- **i102** (primitive-envy-sweep-bluebooks-to-value-objects) is
  the umbrella for the primitive-String fixes. This card is the
  adapter-specific surface within that umbrella.
- **i80** is the retirement contract for pre-bluebook Ruby
  extensions ; these five bluebooks ARE the bluebook side of
  that contract for their respective adapters, so getting them
  clean unlocks the corresponding Ruby retirements.

## Addendum 2026-05-11 — three parity drifts surfaced

When I tried to extend `parity/parity_test.rb`'s CAPS glob to
include `adapters/**/*.bluebook` so the consolidated adapter
bluebooks land in parity coverage, **three** of the now-three
bluebooks under adapters/ failed in the **parity** direction
(Ruby DSL parser disagrees with Rust storehouse parser) :

| Path | Drift surface |
|---|---|
| `adapters/auth/auth.bluebook` | drift (parity mismatch — Ruby + Rust produce different canonical JSON) |
| `adapters/bus/storehouse_api/storehouse_api.bluebook` | `undefined method 'to_sym' for module BaseUrl` (Ruby parser fails) |
| `adapters/storage/r2/r2.bluebook` | `undefined method 'rule' for an instance of Hecks::DSL::ValueObjectBuilder` (Ruby parser fails) |

These are PRE-EXISTING — the originals at
`hecks_conception/adapters/{storehouse_api,r2}.bluebook` have
the same bugs ; they've just never been in the parity scan
(hecks_conception/adapters/ isn't in CAPS). The `adapters/`
consolidation surfaced them.

Two of the three (storehouse_api, r2) are Ruby DSL parser bugs
— the Ruby side doesn't recognize syntax the Rust side accepts
(specifically : `rule "…" do … end` inside a value_object, and
referencing a value-object module type for an attribute). Real
Ruby parser work, not bluebook source work.

The third (auth, the one I just wrote) is a parity-JSON
disagreement ; needs canonical-IR-level diagnosis.

`parity/parity_test.rb` was NOT extended to scan `adapters/`
because adding three drifts to `known_drift.txt` is an explicit
antibody failure per that file's header comment. Adapters/ stays
outside the parity scan until these three are fixed.
