# i555 — Macrophage rule : value_objects inside aggregates, never at bluebook level

## The principle

> "no - duplication is a ok! don't declare them outside" — Chris,
> 2026-05-12

During the antibody.bluebook primitive-envy wrap pass, I first
tried declaring shared value_objects (FilePath, CommitSha,
BranchRef, Verdict, Mode, Result, Reason) at the bluebook level
so the four aggregates (CommitCheck / BranchScan / StagedCheck /
ExemptRegistry) could share them without duplication. Chris's
correction inverted the move : duplication is fine, VOs must
live INSIDE their aggregate.

Why : value_objects are part of an aggregate's vocabulary, not
the bluebook file's. When CommitCheck says `FilePath`, that's
CommitCheck's notion of a path ; when StagedCheck says
`FilePath`, that's StagedCheck's notion. The two MAY share a
name and shape, but they belong to different vocabularies. The
aggregate is the unit of meaning ; the bluebook file is just
its physical home. DDD purity over DRY.

## What to register

A new Check on the Macrophage aggregate (via the existing
RegisterCheck command) :

```
Macrophage.RegisterCheck
  id:           value_objects_outside_aggregate
  pattern_kind: bluebook_top_level_value_object
  severity:     warn
  complaint:    value_object declared at bluebook level — VOs belong inside their aggregate. Duplicate the VO into each aggregate that uses it.
```

The macrophage kernel needs the matching `pattern_kind`
detection : scan a bluebook's source, look for `value_object "..."`
lines that are NOT inside an `aggregate "..."` body, complain.

## Acceptance

- The Check is registered (via fixtures or seed dispatch).
- The macrophage kernel includes `bluebook_top_level_value_object`
  in its pattern_kind dispatcher.
- A test bluebook with a top-level VO triggers the complaint.
