# Gate 9 specializer-golden gap

The 2nd-Futamura specializer (rust/src/specializer/) generates runtime.rs fragments from bluebook IR ; the result is supposed to be byte-identical to the actual rust/src/runtime/mod.rs (Gate 9 in the pre-commit suite). After today's VoiceLatency bluebook landed and the voice/latency.rs module was placed under rust/src/runtime/voice/, mod.rs gained `pub mod voice;` and `voice/latency.rs` — but the specializer doesn't know about these and its golden output is now diverged from the actual file.

## Resolution paths

1. **Add the voice mod to specializer emissions** — update codegen/runtime_mod_shape/ (or wherever the specializer reads its shape) so it emits `pub mod voice;` and the voice/latency submodule when a VoiceLatency aggregate is detected in the bluebook catalog. Cleanest.

2. **Restructure to a module the specializer already emits** — move latency.rs out of voice/ into a top-level module the specializer recognises (e.g. inline in mod.rs or under an existing path). Faster but loses the voice/ grouping.

3. **Drop voice_latency to known_drift** — add Gate 9's relevant fragment to known-drift exceptions until the specializer catches up. Tactical, kicks the can.

## Blocks (cards that can't commit until this resolves)

All i667-i688 plus today's tts_dispatcher.rs edits (mpg123.wait + cancel_prior_render disable) currently sit unstaged-but-locally-modified waiting on this.

## Scope

~1-2 hours to do option 1 properly. Less to do options 2 or 3.
