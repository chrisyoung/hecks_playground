### i752 — fixed, closed

`bin/project_rust` used to `Kernel.load` exactly one basename-matched
file, silently dropping the rest of a multi-file domain. Now routes
through `Ports::Loading.bootstrap` the same way `Loader#boot` does.
Verified against `conductor` (5 aggregates) and `framework/agent` (3
co-located domains).

### i753 — fixed, closed

A scalar default/literal headed at a VO-typed field (`SlideNumber`,
`Status`) emitted bare (`Some(1)`) instead of wrapped
(`Some(SlideNumber { value: 1 })`). New `scalar_literal_rhs` helper in
`bridging.rb`, reused across both call sites.

### i754 — fixed, closed (the big one)

The lifecycle field was hardcoded as "always a bare String" across six
codegen sites (fielded.rb, types.rb, domain_generator.rb, commands.rb,
mutations.rb) — but it's a normal declared attribute, often VO-typed
(Agent/AuthIdentity/Role's `Status`, a closed-set enum). Fixed with a
new `lifecycle_field_declared?` guard everywhere the duplicate/wrong-
typed emission fired, plus extended `scalar_literal_rhs` to wrap
closed-set enum literals too (`"pending"` -> `Status::Pending`).
`framework/agent` went from 37 compile errors to 0 attributable to
this bug. `demo`/`conductor` regression-checked clean, 0 errors both
before and after.

## OPEN — not blockers, just real, undecided items

- **i755** (filed this session, NOT fixed): an unconstrained lifecycle
  transition (`transition "Retire" => "retired"`, no `from:`) renders
  as a bare Rust `nil` inside a `&[&str]` slice — doesn't compile.
  Surfaced verifying i754, unrelated root cause (VO-typing is fine;
  this is about `TransitionCheck` having no "any current state"
  representation). Real design question: change `TransitionCheck`'s
  shape (e.g. `Option<&[&str]>`), or enumerate closed-set members at
  generation time (doesn't generalize to free-form String lifecycle
  fields). `framework/agent` is currently the ONLY domain that hits
  this (Agent.Retire, Role.Retire) — `demo`/`conductor` unaffected.
- **i751** (older, untouched this session): `catalog`'s
  `Explorer.ByKind` query asks for a top-level `kind` field that only
  exists nested inside `ExplorerEntry` — a real query-semantics design
  question (list-filter vs. new top-level field), not a quick fix.
- **i748** (older, untouched this session): three meta-layer DSL verbs
  (`behavior_kind`, `adapter_family`, `provider`) are designed in the
  corpus's own IR bluebook but never ported to `vendor/hecksagain`,
  invisible to both `validate` and `.behaviors` gates.
